# Entombed Sentinels handover reference

The maintained encounter definition now lives in
[`docs/entombed-sentinels-encounter.md`](../../docs/entombed-sentinels-encounter.md).
That document is the only implementation contract and is intended to be
iterated as live evidence improves.

This handover entry records why Sentinels remains an architecture reference:

- two simultaneously active bosses and raid sides;
- independent player, NPC, enemy, projectile, aura, and arena timelines;
- accumulation with independent expiry;
- group soaks, persistent pool placement, dispels, pairing, and side swaps;
- the same mechanic vocabulary projected through separate Learn 2D and Train
  3D schedules and arena models.

The supplied research synthesis is preserved at
[`inbox/entombed-sentinels-wow-trainer-spec.md`](../../inbox/entombed-sentinels-wow-trainer-spec.md).
It is evidence, not a parallel specification. The canonical document records
all deliberate trainer abstractions and unresolved confidence boundaries.
