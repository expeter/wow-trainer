# Source material

- `scenario-blueprint.json` is the structured Season 2 encounter/module
  blueprint researched as of 2026-08-13.
- `raid-research.md` is the corresponding long-form research and source index.
- `survey-summary.md` is an anonymized synthesis of the August 2026 guild
  survey.

The raid had not opened on live EU servers at the research cutoff. Journal
constants and PTR observations must be treated as versioned provisional timing,
not silently promoted to authoritative live behavior.

The private raw survey contains names and attributed free text. It is not part
of this folder and must not be committed to a public repository. If it is
needed locally, place it under `source-material/private/`, which is ignored by
the handover ignore rule.
